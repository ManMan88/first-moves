## The idea

Most songs are built from **sections**. A verse, a chorus, a quiet break, a big ending. Each section is a mix of parts: maybe the chorus has busier drums, while the bass stays the same.

On Move, each part is a [clip](/first-moves/glossary/#clip). **Session Mode** puts all of them on the pads at once, as you saw in [Lesson 4](/first-moves/lessons/04-three-views/). Each row of pads belongs to one track, and each pad in that row is a slot where one of its clips can live. A column of pads, one slot from each track, is a [scene](/first-moves/glossary/#scene). Think of a scene as one section of your song: start the scene and all its clips play together.

<SetMap />

The pad lights tell you what is where:

- A **dark** pad is an empty slot.
- A pad lit in the **track's colour** holds a clip.
- A **white** pad is an empty slot you have selected, ready for a new clip.
- A pad **pulsing white** is the clip that is selected on that track (the one that plays).
- A pad **pulsing green** is waiting to start. A pad **pulsing in the track's colour** is about to stop.

Why wait? When the music is already running, a clip you launch doesn't jump in straight away. It waits for the start of the next bar, so the new part lands in time with everything else. You can press a little early and let Move do the timing.

Each track plays only one clip at a time. So when you start a new clip in a row, the old one in that row stops.

<Steps>
  <Step press="note-session" see="The pads show your clips: one row per track, lit pads for clips, dark pads for empty slots.">
    Press **Note/Session** to switch to Session Mode. Look at the pads before you press any of them.
    <Tip>If the pads play sounds instead, you were already in Session Mode and have just switched to Note Mode. Press **Note/Session** once more.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="pad-1-2" see="The pad turns white: an empty slot, selected and waiting for a new clip.">
    Find a column where **every** pad is dark. Press its pad in the row of your drum track. The drawing shows one example; your empty column may be in a different place.
    <Tip>Each row lines up with the track button beside it. In a new Set, the drum kit is on the top track. If the music was playing, the drum clip in this row now pulses in the track's colour, which means it is about to stop. That is normal: an empty slot is now the drums' selected clip.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="track-1" see="Move switches to Note Mode on the drum track, ready to fill the empty slot.">
    Press the **track button** beside that row. This opens Note Mode, ready to fill the new clip.
    <Tip>Pressing **Note/Session** works too. Either way, the notes you add next go into the slot you just picked.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="drum-pads steps" hear="A new drum pattern, different from your first beat.">
    Build a new beat, just as in [Lesson 9](/first-moves/lessons/09-first-beat/): press a drum pad, then press some **step buttons**. Make it clearly different, for example busier hi-hats and an extra snare, like a chorus.
    <Tip>If the music isn't running, press **Play** to hear your pattern. Keep it simple: two or three drum sounds are plenty for now.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="note-session" see="Session Mode again. The slot you picked is now lit in the drum track's colour: it holds your new clip.">
    Press **Note/Session** to go back to Session Mode.
  </Step>
  <Step press="pad-1-1" see="The pad you pressed pulses green, then takes over at the start of the next bar.">
    Press the pad of your **first** drum clip, in the same row. Listen for the moment it switches.
    <Tip>Press the new clip's pad to switch back the same way. Nothing waits if Move was stopped: pressing a lit pad then starts the music straight away. If you want to stop, press **Play**.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="copy pad-2-1" hold="copy" see="The display confirms the copy, then asks where to paste it.">
    Now give the new column a bassline too. Hold **Copy** and press a lit pad in the **bass** row: the bassline you want to reuse. If the bass row has several clips, pick your favourite.
    <Tip>Changed your mind? Press **Copy** again to empty the clipboard.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="pad-2-2" see="The pad lights up in the bass track's colour: a copy of the clip is now in that slot.">
    Let go of **Copy**. In the bass row, press the **dark** pad in the same column as your new drum clip.
    <Tip>Pasting onto a lit pad replaces the clip that was there. If that happens by accident, press **Undo** straight away.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="column-1" hear="On the next bar, every clip in that column starts together: your first section.">
    While the music plays, pick a column that holds your **older** clips, with several rows lit (the drawing shows one example). Slide one finger quickly down it, from the top pad to the bottom one.
    <Tip>Make it one smooth, quick slide, and do it while the music is running. If Move is stopped, press **Play** first.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="column-2" hear="On the next bar, the section changes: new drums, same bass.">
    Now slide down the column with your **new** drum clip and the bass copy. You have just switched from one scene to the next, like a song moving into its chorus.
    <Tip>Slide back and forth between the two columns a few times, a little before the end of a bar. Getting a feel for the timing is the whole trick.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="play" hear="Silence.">
    Press **Play** to stop the music.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Try it yourself

- Open the copied bass clip in Note Mode (select its pad, then press **Note/Session**) and change a few notes, so the second section has its own bassline.
- Build a third scene in the next column: a break with only drums and chords. Then play your scenes in order: first, second, break, first again.
- While the music plays, slide down a column of dark pads. Every clip stops, but the music clock keeps running, so the next scene you start still comes in on time.

## Good to know

- To delete a clip, hold **Delete** and press its pad. It's gone at once, so keep **Undo** in mind.
- You don't have to fill a slot from Note Mode by sequencing. Recording and Capture ([Lesson 11](/first-moves/lessons/11-record-live/) and [Lesson 12](/first-moves/lessons/12-capture/)) put their notes into the selected slot too.
- A scene can mix clips from all four tracks, but each row still plays only one clip at a time.

<Note kind="newer" firmware="2.0.5">
  If you launch a clip so close to the start of a bar that Move can't switch over smoothly, it now lets that bar go by and starts the clip on the following one. Press a little earlier if you want the very next bar.
</Note>

<Note kind="newer" firmware="2.0">
  Some Shift shortcuts belong to Note Mode only. Key & Scale and Full Velocity are hidden while you're in Session Mode. Switch back to Note Mode to change them.
</Note>
