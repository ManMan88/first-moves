## The idea

Move keeps everything you make inside itself: your Sets, the sounds you record, and the presets you save. That's great on the go, but sooner or later you'll want to get things **off** Move (a safety copy of a song you love) or **onto** it (a sound you found or made somewhere else).

That's the job of **Move Manager**. It isn't an app you install. Move runs a small web page of its own, and you open it in an ordinary web browser, like Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge, on a computer, phone or tablet. From there you can:

- **Download Sets**, so you have a backup on your computer.
- **Upload samples**. A [sample](/first-moves/glossary/#sample) is a short recording, like a drum hit or a voice, that you can play from a pad.
- Look after your recordings and saved presets, and update Move's software.

Your browser and Move need a way to reach each other. There are two:

- Both are on the **same Wi-Fi network**. This is the easy way if Move already has Wi-Fi from [Lesson 2](/first-moves/lessons/02-power-and-setup/). A phone on mobile data, or a laptop on a different network, won't find Move.
- A **USB-C cable** from Move's back to your computer.

To find Move, the browser needs Move's **hostname**: a short web address that Move shows you in its Setup menu. The first time you connect, Move also shows a code on its display, to prove that the Move in your hands is the one you're talking to.

<Steps view="both">
  <Step press="usb-c" see="Move and your computer can now reach each other.">
    Choose how to connect. Either check that Move and your computer are on the same Wi-Fi network, or plug the **USB-C** cable into Move's back and into your computer.
    <Tip>Not sure if Move is on Wi-Fi? [Lesson 2](/first-moves/lessons/02-power-and-setup/) shows how to check it and join a network. On an office or school network, devices are sometimes kept apart from each other; the cable is the simpler choice there.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="shift step-2" hold="shift" see="The Setup menu opens.">
    Hold **Shift**, then press **Step 2**. This brings up the Setup menu.
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The Move Manager menu opens, with a short list of its own.">
    Turn **the wheel** to the **Move Manager** entry and press the wheel to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The display shows Move's hostname: the address to type into your browser.">
    Turn **the wheel** to **How to access?** and press the wheel. Note down the address you see. It usually ends in **.local**, for example move.local.
    <Tip summary="A different name?">If several Moves share one Wi-Fi network, each one gets its own name, such as a number added after "move". That's fine: always use the name your own Move shows here.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="display" see="Move Manager's home page opens in your browser.">
    On your computer, open a web browser and type the hostname into the address bar, then press Enter. The browser asks for a code, and a code appears on Move's **display**. Type the code from the display into the browser.
    <Tip>Page not loading? Check the spelling of the hostname, and that Move and the computer really are on the same network. If the browser runs a web search instead, type **http://** in front of the name. Next time, the browser may let you straight in without a new code.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="back" hold="back" see="The Setup menu closes. Move Manager stays open in your browser.">
    Hold **Back** to close the Setup menu on Move. Now make a backup in the browser: open the **Sets** page, which lists every Set on Move by name, and click the download icon next to the Set you want to keep. The file goes to your computer's Downloads folder.
    <Tip summary="Setup already gone?">If Move no longer shows the Setup menu, skip the Back press and just do the backup in the browser.</Tip>
    <Tip summary="Download blocked in Chrome?">Chrome may say the download is insecure and block it. That's only because Move's address ends in .local. Choose **Keep** to let the file through.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="track-1" see="The top track is selected, and the display shows its sound.">
    Now bring a sound in. In the browser, open the **Samples** page, click **Upload**, and pick your sound file. You can also drag the file onto the page. When the upload has finished, go back to Move and press the **top track button**. In a brand-new Set, the top track holds a drum kit.
    <Tip>Move takes WAV, AIF and AIFF files of up to 100 MB each. An MP3 won't upload: convert it to WAV first. If your top track isn't a drum kit (a demo Set, or a sound you swapped), [Lesson 8](/first-moves/lessons/08-choosing-sounds/) shows how to load one. If the display doesn't show that track's sound yet, press the top track button once more.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="drum-pads" hear="The sound you're about to replace.">
    Tap the drum pad whose sound you want to swap for yours. The pad you tap last is the selected one, just like in [Lesson 9](/first-moves/lessons/09-first-beat/).
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The browser opens on a list of drum sounds, and the pad you picked pulses.">
    Turn **the wheel** until only the sample's icon is highlighted and the display shows the name of a sample, not the kit's name. Then press **the wheel** to open the browser.
    <Tip>If every icon is highlighted, the whole kit is selected, and the browser would swap the entire kit. If the display shows an effect such as Reverb or Delay, you're on an effect. Turn the wheel one click at a time until a single icon is highlighted and a sample name appears.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="back" see="The main list of categories.">
    Press **Back** once to leave this folder of drum sounds and see all the categories, as in [Lesson 8](/first-moves/lessons/08-choosing-sounds/).
    <Tip>You can also scroll to the very top of the list and press the wheel on the Back entry. Pressed **Back** twice and landed on the icons again? The browser has closed. Press **the wheel** to open it again.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" hear="Your own sound, playing from the pad you picked.">
    Turn **the wheel** to **User Samples** and press the wheel to open it. Turn to your file, then tap your pad to hear it. When it's the one you want, press **the wheel** to keep it and close the browser.
    <Tip>Don't see your file? Check that the upload finished in the browser, and that you opened **User Samples**, not User Recordings. Changed your mind? Press **Undo**.</Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Try it yourself

- Back up every Set you care about. Keep the downloaded files in one folder on your computer.
- Record a few sounds on your phone (a door slam, a cup tapped with a spoon, a word), save them as WAV, and build your own drum kit from them.
- Play your new sample at different pitches: hold **Shift** and press **Step 8** to turn on the 16 Pitches layout ([Lesson 6](/first-moves/lessons/06-playing-pads/) tried it), and play the right-hand pads.

## Good to know

- **Other pages.** **Recordings** lists the audio you've recorded on Move, so you can play, download or rename it. **Presets** holds the sounds you saved with Save Preset in [Lesson 8](/first-moves/lessons/08-choosing-sounds/); give them better names here. **Settings** shows free space and your software version, and has an **Update Now** button when new software is out. Over the cable, updating this way works even without Wi-Fi.
- **Deleting is for keeps.** Deleting a Set, sample, recording or preset in Move Manager can't be undone.
- **Folders.** On the Samples page, **New Folder** helps you sort sounds, for example into kicks, snares and voices. To fill a folder, open it first, then upload into it.
- **Logs for support.** If Ableton's support team ever asks for logs, add **/advanced** to the end of the hostname in your browser to reach a hidden page where you can download them.
- **Opening Sets elsewhere.** A downloaded Set is an .ablbundle file. Ableton Live and the Note app on iPhone or iPad can open it.

<Note kind="newer" firmware="1.6">
  Since Move 1.6 you can also send a Set **back** to Move. On the **Sets** page, use the **Upload** button, or drag a Set file from your computer onto the list. That's how you restore a backup.
</Note>

<Note kind="newer" firmware="1.8">
  Each Set can use a limited amount of sample memory. The manual gives 400 MB; since Move 1.8 the limit is 800 MB per Set. Short drum hits use very little, so you'll rarely notice it.
</Note>
