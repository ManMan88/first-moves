## The idea

A **Set** is one song or idea on your Move. Every Set has four tracks, and each track plays its own sound. The [Set Overview](/first-moves/glossary/#set-overview) is the place where all your Sets live. Here the 32 pads turn into 32 storage slots: a pad lit in a colour holds a Set, and a dark pad is a free slot. So your Move can keep up to 32 Sets.

To start something new, you pick a free slot and step into it. Move then fills the four tracks for you with random sounds (called [Track Presets](/first-moves/glossary/#track-preset)). You always get one drum kit, one bass, and two other melodic sounds, but which ones is a surprise every time. That is a nice way to get unstuck: a new Set is a new box of toys.

There is no save button. Move keeps your work safe on its own: it saves as you go, again each time you open the Set Overview, and once more when you turn it off.

In this lesson you will make a Set, colour its pad, make a copy of it, and then delete that copy. The copy is a safe thing to practise deleting on.

<Steps>
  <Step press="shift step-1" hold="shift" see="The Set Overview opens and the pads show your Sets.">
    Hold **Shift**, then press **Step 1**.
    <Tip>Keep Shift held down the whole time you press Step 1. If you let go of Shift first, Step 1 does its normal job instead.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="pad-3-1" see="The pad you chose lights up white, which means an empty slot is selected.">
    Press a pad that is dark. Any unlit pad will do; the drawing just marks one example.
    <Tip>A lit pad already holds a Set. If you pressed one of those, simply press a dark pad instead.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="tracks" see="Move leaves the Set Overview and opens your brand-new Set in Note Mode.">
    Press any one of the **track buttons** on the left of the pads.
  </Step>
  <Step press="pads" hear="Each pad plays part of whatever sound this track was given.">
    Tap a few pads to hear what this track sounds like.
    <Tip>Press a different track button and tap the pads again. Each of the four tracks got its own random sound, so each one sounds different.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="shift step-1" hold="shift" see="You are back in the Set Overview. Your new Set's pad now has a colour, and it pulses because it is the Set you have selected.">
    Hold **Shift** and press **Step 1** again.
  </Step>
  <Step press="shift pad-3-1" hold="shift" see="The display shows the options for this Set, including an entry called Color.">
    Hold **Shift**, then press your new Set's pad.
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The Color entry is chosen and you can now pick a colour.">
    Press **the wheel** while Color is showing.
    <Tip>If a different entry is showing, turn the wheel until you reach Color, then press it.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The Set's pad changes colour as you turn.">
    Turn **the wheel** slowly and stop on a colour you like.
  </Step>
  <Step press="back" see="You are back in the Set Overview, and the pad keeps its new colour.">
    Press **Back**.
  </Step>
  <Step press="copy pad-3-1" hold="copy" see="Move now remembers this Set, ready to be placed somewhere else.">
    Hold **Copy**, then press your Set's pad.
    <Tip>Changed your mind? Press **Copy** once more to forget what you copied.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="pad-3-2" see="A second coloured pad appears. It holds a copy of your Set.">
    Let go of Copy. Then press a dark pad to put the copy there.
    <Tip>Aim for a dark pad. If you press a pad that already holds a Set, the display asks you to confirm, and a second press would replace that Set for good. Undo cannot bring it back.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="delete pad-3-2" hold="delete" see="The display asks you to confirm the delete.">
    Hold **Delete**, then press the pad of the copy you just made.
  </Step>
  <Step press="pad-3-2" see="The copy is gone, and its slot is free for a new Set.">
    Press that same pad once more to confirm.
    <Tip>Check that it is the copy's pad before this second press. A deleted Set cannot be restored.</Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Try it yourself

- Make one more new Set and listen to all four tracks. Compare its sounds with your first Set: they will almost certainly be different.
- Give each of your Sets its own colour, so you can spot them at a glance.
- Tap a Set's pad in the Set Overview, then press **Play** to hear what is in it. Press **Play** again to stop.

## Good to know

<Note kind="info" title="Deleting is for keeps">
  Move cannot bring back a deleted Set, and pasting a copy over an existing Set replaces it with no way back. When you want to try a risky change, copy the Set first and experiment on the copy.
</Note>

- To leave the Set Overview and go back to what you were doing, press **Back**.
- You can keep up to 32 Sets. When all 32 pads are lit, delete a Set you no longer need to free a slot.
