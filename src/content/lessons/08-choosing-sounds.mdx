## The idea

Every track on Move has one sound, called a **Track Preset**. A preset is a ready-made starting point. You can play it as it is, change it with the knobs, or swap it for a different one whenever you like. A new Set gets random presets, so every Set starts out a little different.

Each Track Preset is made of two kinds of parts:

- An **instrument** makes the sound. On Move it's either a synthesizer (Drift or Wavetable), a **drum kit** (called a Drum Rack), or a sampler that plays a recording at different pitches (Melodic Sampler).
- Two **effects** then change the sound, for example reverb (a sense of space), delay (echoes) or a filter (brighter or darker tone).

When you press a track button, the display shows a small icon for each of these parts (sampler sounds get one extra icon, for the recording they play), and you can step through them with **the wheel**. The **browser** is the list of all the sounds you can load. It's sorted into categories such as Drums, Pad or Strings, so you can quickly find the kind of sound you want.

<Steps>
  <Step press="tracks" see="The display shows the track's preset: a row of small icons and the preset's name.">
    Press one of the **track buttons**. This takes you to the sound of that track.
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="All the icons are highlighted, and the display shows the preset's name.">
    Turn **the wheel** until all the icons are highlighted together. That means the whole preset is selected, not just one part of it.
    <Tip>If only one icon is highlighted, you've selected a single part (such as an effect). Turn the wheel back and forth until they all light up.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The browser opens in the preset's category, and all the pads pulse.">
    Press **the wheel** to open the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="You move to the next preset in the list, and it loads straight away.">
    Turn **the wheel** one click to move to the next preset in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step press="pads" hear="The new sound.">
    Play some **pads** to hear it. Keep turning the wheel and playing to try a few more. Each one loads as soon as you reach it.
    <Tip>A drum kit plays on the left 16 pads, one drum sound per pad. A melodic sound uses all 32 pads, and the notes get higher as you go to the right.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="back" see="The main list of categories.">
    Press **Back** once to leave this category and see all the categories.
    <Tip>You can also scroll to the very top of the list and press the wheel on the Back entry there. Pressed **Back** twice and landed on the icons again? The browser has closed. Press **the wheel** to open it again.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The presets in that category.">
    Turn **the wheel** to another category, such as Drums or Pad, and press **the wheel** to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The browser closes and you're back to the track's icons, with your new sound loaded.">
    When you've found a sound you like, press **the wheel** to keep it and close the browser.
    <Tip>Changed your mind? Press **Undo** to go back to the previous sound.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="encoders" hear="The sound changes as you turn.">
    Turn a few of the **knobs** to change the sound. Touch a knob to see on the display what it changes.
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The browser opens again, with your tweaked preset still loaded.">
    To save your version, make sure all the icons are still highlighted, then press **the wheel** to open the browser. Don't turn the wheel yet.
    <Tip>If you turn the wheel in the browser now, a different preset loads and your changes are gone. If that happens, press **Undo**.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="shift wheel" hold="shift" see="The Options menu opens.">
    Hold **Shift**, then press **the wheel**.
    <Tip>Holding Shift shows an Options entry in the browser. If pressing the wheel while holding Shift doesn't open it, wait until Options shows, let go of Shift, and then press the wheel.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="Your preset is saved.">
    Turn **the wheel** to **Save Preset** and press **the wheel** to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Try it yourself

- Open a few more categories and play some presets in each.
- Give every track a new sound: find one drum kit and three melodic sounds that you like together.
- Open the **Templates** category. Its presets are plain starting points for building a sound from scratch. Load one and see how far the knobs can take it.
- Find the preset you saved in the **User Presets** category.

## Good to know

- Look for your saved sounds in the **User Presets** folder of the browser. Their names come from the instrument or effect you began with. To give one a better name, or to throw it away, use Move Manager on a computer.
- You can open the browser on a single effect too: highlight just that effect's icon and press the wheel. Then you're browsing settings for that effect only.
- Sounds change the moment you scroll to them because a setting called **Autoload** is on. You can switch it off in Workflow Settings: hold **Shift**, press **Step 3**, find the Autoload entry with the wheel, and press the wheel. After that, scrolling only picks a preset, and a press of the wheel is what swaps the sound in.

<Note kind="newer" firmware="2.0">
  The main list of categories (where **Back** takes you from inside a category) now starts with an **Audio Track** entry at the very top. Choosing it turns the track into one that plays recorded audio instead of an instrument. You'll learn about audio tracks later in the course.
</Note>
