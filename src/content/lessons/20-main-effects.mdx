## The idea

Every track's sound already has two [effects](/first-moves/glossary/#effect) of its own, as you saw in [Lesson 8](/first-moves/lessons/08-choosing-sounds/). On top of that, every Set has **two main effects** that work on the **whole mix**: all four tracks at once. In a new Set they are **Dynamics** (which evens out loud and quiet moments) and **Saturator** (which adds warmth and grit). Demo Sets may use others.

Because they work on everything, main effects are great for moments that change the whole song. Turn a knob slowly and the whole song gets darker, brighter or more crunchy. That's the classic **build-up**: the music gets more and more tense, and then a **drop** releases it, when the full beat crashes back in.

**Performing** on Move means choosing, live, what plays when. You already have the tools:

- **Scenes** switch sections, and they wait for the next bar, so you stay in time ([Lesson 18](/first-moves/lessons/18-session-mode/)).
- **Mute** takes a part out and brings it back ([Lesson 19](/first-moves/lessons/19-mixing/)).
- **Shift** and **Play** together restart every playing clip from its beginning. Pressed a few times in a row, it makes a stuttering, skipping effect.

<Steps>
  <Step press="note-session" see="Session Mode: your clips on the pads, one row per track.">
    Press **Note/Session** to go to Session Mode. The main effects live here.
    <Tip>If the pads now play sounds, you were already in Session Mode. Press **Note/Session** once more.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="play" hear="Your selected clips play.">
    Press **Play**. Effects are easiest to judge while the music runs.
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The display shows which main effect you have picked.">
    Turn **the wheel** to pick one of the two main effects.
  </Step>
  <Step press="encoders" hear="The whole mix changes, all tracks at once.">
    Touch a **knob** to see on the display what it changes, then turn it slowly. Try a few knobs. When you're done, turn each one back to where it was, or leave it if you like the new sound.
    <Tip>Some knobs make only a small difference, especially on Dynamics. Try the other main effect too: turn **the wheel** to pick it, then turn its knobs.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="column-1" hear="On the next bar, your first scene plays.">
    Now perform. Slide one finger quickly down the column of your **older** clips from Lesson 18, from top to bottom.
    <Tip>It must be one quick slide, while the music plays. If Move stopped, press **Play** first.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="mute track-1" hold="mute" hear="The drums drop out. Everything else keeps playing.">
    Take the drums out: hold **Mute** and press the **track button** of your drum track.
    <Tip>Keep holding Mute until you've pressed the track button. While the drums are gone, try slowly turning a main effect knob to build tension.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="mute track-1" hold="mute" hear="The drums crash back in: the drop.">
    Right as a new bar starts, hold **Mute** and press the drum track button again.
    <Tip>Count the beats along with the music, "1, 2, 3, 4", and press as you say "1". It takes a bit of practice, so try it a few times.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="column-2" hear="On the next bar, the song moves into your second section.">
    Slide down the column with your **new** drum clip and the bass copy from Lesson 18.
  </Step>
  <Step press="shift play" hold="shift" hear="All the playing clips jump back to their beginning, right away.">
    Hold **Shift** and press **Play**. Try tapping **Play** two or three times in a row while you keep Shift down, for a stuttering effect.
    <Tip>Without Shift, Play stops the music. If that happens, press **Play** again and carry on.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="column-8" hear="All the clips stop, but the music clock keeps running.">
    To end, slide down a column of **dark** pads. The drawing shows the column on the far right; use any column where every pad is dark.
    <Tip>Because the clock is still running, a scene you start now still comes in right on the bar. That's handy for a clean restart.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="play" hear="Silence.">
    Press **Play** to stop the clock. Your performance is done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Try it yourself

Plan a one-minute performance and play it through:

1. Start with the first scene, drums muted.
2. Unmute the drums on a bar start.
3. Switch to the second scene, and slowly turn up a main effect knob for a build-up.
4. Mute the drums for one bar, then bring them back for the drop.
5. End by sliding over an empty column.

## Good to know

- You can swap each main effect for a different one from Move's browser, such as Reverb, Delay, Auto Filter or Redux. The choices are the same effects that Track Presets use.
- Shift and Play restart clips in Note Mode too, not only in Session Mode.
