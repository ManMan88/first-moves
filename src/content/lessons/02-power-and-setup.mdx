## The idea

Move runs on a battery, so you can make music on the sofa, on a train or in bed. A full charge lasts for up to about four hours of playing. You charge it through the USB-C port on the back, either with the power supply from the box or from a computer.

Most of Move's housekeeping lives in one place: the **Setup menu**. It's where you check the battery, dim the lights, join Wi-Fi and install new software. You open it with a Shift shortcut, and you move around it with the **wheel**:

- **Turn** the wheel to move the highlight up and down the list.
- **Press** the wheel to open the highlighted entry, or to switch it.
- Press **Back** to go one level back out.

That same turn, press and Back pattern works in almost every menu and list on Move, so it's worth getting comfortable with it now.

<Steps view="both">
  <Step press="power" see="The display wakes up and Move starts.">
    To switch Move on, find the yellow **Power button** on its back edge and press it.
    <Tip>If nothing happens, the battery may be empty. Plug in the USB-C cable (see the next steps) and try again after a few minutes.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The Set Overview, where your songs are kept.">
    The very first time you switch Move on, a short welcome setup appears. It offers to join Wi-Fi, turn on error reporting and install the newest software. You can follow it, or turn the **wheel** to **Skip** and press the wheel. Skipping is fine: this lesson covers the same things.
    <Tip summary="No welcome setup?">It only appears on the first start. If your Move went straight to a screen of Sets, there's nothing to skip. Move on to the next step.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="usb-c" see="A short message on the display says Move is charging.">
    Plug the USB-C cable into the **USB-C** port on the back, and the other end into the power supply or a computer.
    <Tip>If Move is switched off when you plug it in, you'll see a battery symbol for a moment instead.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="shift step-2" hold="shift" see="The Setup menu opens, and the icon under Step 2 flashes while it's open.">
    Hold **Shift**, then press **Step 2**. Let go of both.
    <Tip>Press them in that order: Shift first, and keep holding it while you press Step 2.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The highlight moves through the list, and the battery level appears next to the Battery entry.">
    Turn the **wheel** slowly to scroll through the list until you reach **Battery**. Entries with an arrow open a smaller menu. Entries with a dash switch something on or off.
    <Tip summary="Want more detail?">Pressing the wheel on Battery shows what the battery is doing, for example Charging. Press **Back** afterwards to return to the list.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The Brightness entry opens.">
    Turn the **wheel** on to **Brightness**, then press the wheel to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The pads and buttons get brighter or dimmer as you turn.">
    Turn the **wheel** to try the different brightness levels, and stop on one you like.
    <Tip>This setting changes the lights in the pads and buttons, not the display. At the very top level, Max, only the pads get brighter than at High.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="back" see="You're back in the Setup list.">
    Press **Back** to return to the list.
    <Tip summary="Did the whole menu close?">If Back took you all the way out of Setup, that's fine. Reopen it the same way as before (**Shift** plus **Step 2**), then carry on.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The Wi-Fi menu opens, showing Wi-Fi Off if Wi-Fi isn't on yet.">
    Turn the **wheel** to **Wi-Fi** and press it to open. If you don't want to use Wi-Fi now, press **Back** and skip ahead to the step that closes the Setup menu.
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="Wi-Fi Off changes to Wi-Fi On, and a list of nearby networks appears.">
    With **Wi-Fi Off** highlighted, press the **wheel** to switch Wi-Fi on.
    <Tip summary="Already says Wi-Fi On?">If Wi-Fi is already on and your network has a check mark, you're connected. Don't press the wheel here, or you'll switch Wi-Fi off. Skip ahead to the step that leaves the Wi-Fi menu.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="Once you're connected, your network has a check mark next to its name.">
    Turn the **wheel** to your network and press it. If it asks for a password, type it in (see the tip).
    <Tip summary="Typing the password">Move shows a row of letters. Turn the wheel to a letter and press it to type it; press again to type it twice. Hold **Shift** for capital letters. The small three-dot icon next to the space bar switches to capitals, then numbers and symbols. Use the backspace icon or **Back** to delete a letter. When you're done, select the check mark in the bottom right corner and press the wheel.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="back" see="You're back in the Setup list.">
    Press **Back** to leave the Wi-Fi menu.
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="The Update menu, with Install and a version number if new software is ready.">
    Turn the **wheel** to **Update** and press it. If you see **Install**, select it, press the wheel and follow what the display tells you. Let the update finish before you carry on. If Move restarts after updating, skip the next step.
    <Tip summary="No Install option?">Updating from Move needs Wi-Fi, so join a network first. If you're connected, you're probably up to date. **Current Version** shows which version you have. If your Move shows a **Check Update** entry, use it to look for a new version. A small asterisk next to Update in the Setup list means a new version is ready.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="back" hold="back" see="The Setup menu closes and you're back where you started.">
    Hold **Back** to leave the Setup menu in one go.
    <Tip>Pressing **Back** works too. From the main list one press is enough; if you're deeper, inside a smaller menu, press it twice.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="power" see="Move doesn't switch off yet. It waits for you to confirm.">
    When you've finished, press the **Power button** on the back to start switching off.
    <Tip>Changed your mind? Press **Back** and Move stays on.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step press="wheel" see="Move saves your work and switches off.">
    Press the **wheel** to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note kind="newer" firmware="1.7">
  Since Move 1.7, the Brightness entry has two extra-low levels, **Dim** and **Min**. They are handy in a dark room.
</Note>

## Try it yourself

- Switch Move on, open Setup and find the battery level without looking at this page.
- Set the brightness to the lowest level, then the highest. Which one would you use at night?
- Leave the Setup menu using a different way from the one you used above.

## Good to know

- Move saves your music by itself, including when you switch it off. If the battery runs out, it saves and shuts down safely, and warns you on the display when the battery gets low.
- If Move ever freezes, hold the **Power button** for ten seconds. That forces it to switch off.
- Once Wi-Fi is on, Move joins your network by itself every time it starts.
